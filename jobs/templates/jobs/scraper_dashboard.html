How It Works

  1. Enter a job keyword and location
  2. Select which job sites to scrape
  3. Click "Run Scraper" and wait
  4. Review scraped jobs and PESO vacancy-category leads below
  5. Publish only verified individual job advertisements. PESO counts are review-only.

Note: Scraped job listings and PESO category leads are saved separately within scrape records. Use the "Publish" button to convert them into visible job postings.
