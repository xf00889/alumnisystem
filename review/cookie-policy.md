# Cookie Policy

## How we use cookies

Cookies are small pieces of information that help a website remember things. NORSU Alumni Network uses some cookies to keep your account working. Others are optional and help us understand how the website is used.

## Cookies the website needs

We use necessary cookies to keep you signed in securely, protect forms and remember your cookie choice. These do not require you to accept optional visitor statistics. Your privacy choice is normally remembered for up to 180 days. Login cookies follow the University's account security settings.

## Optional visitor statistics

If you choose **Accept analytics**, and visitor tracking is enabled, we use a temporary browser identifier to estimate first-time and returning visits. This identifier is normally kept on your browser for up to 30 days. We may also collect your internet address (IP address), date of visit and a limited count of page visits. One browser or internet address does not always mean one person. Only authorized administrators can view detailed visitor records.

Optional visitor records are configured to be removed after up to 30 days when routine cleanup is running. Some security and account records may have a different retention period.

## Your choice

Choose **Necessary only** to use the website without optional visitor statistics. Choose **Accept analytics** to allow them. You can use **Cookie settings** at any time to change your decision. Turning off optional tracking stops future records from this feature and removes its browser identifier. It does not automatically erase older records; you can make a privacy request if needed.

## Notifications on your phone or computer

If you choose to receive alerts, your device and the services that deliver notifications will handle the information needed to send them. This permission is separate from cookie choices. You can turn alerts off in the app or your device settings.

## Outside services

Some features, such as Google sign-in, checks against unwanted activity and linked websites, may use outside providers. They may have their own privacy practices. Optional analytics run by an outside provider must be controlled separately from NORSU's own visitor statistics; the University should review those settings before enabling them.

## Questions

Read our [Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy/) or [Data Privacy Request](/legal/data-privacy-request/) page, or contact the Office of Alumni Affairs through the website.
