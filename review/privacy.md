# Privacy

## Who looks after your information

Negros Oriental State University, through the Office of Alumni Affairs, manages the NORSU Alumni Network and is responsible for how personal information is handled. You can contact the University using the official website details or the [Data Privacy Request](/legal/data-privacy-request/) page.

## What information we collect

Depending on what you use, we may collect:

- your name, email address, profile photograph and account details;
- your campus, degree program, graduation year and proof of alumni status;
- the contact, education, employment and other profile details you choose to provide;
- messages, connections, group activity, announcements and uploaded files;
- event, survey, mentorship, job application and tracer-study information;
- donation records, including payment references and proof needed for review; and
- information needed to protect the service, such as your internet address, device details and records of account activity.

If you agree to optional visitor statistics, and that feature is enabled, we may also record when a browser visits and its internet address (IP address). We use these details to estimate visits, not to guarantee the identity of an individual visitor.

## Why we use this information

We use personal information to confirm accounts, connect alumni, provide the features members request, process donations and applications, send important service updates, prepare authorized university reports, improve the service, and protect members against misuse. We handle it only when there is an appropriate legal reason to do so, including consent where required.

## Who can see information you share

Some profile details are visible to authorized members, depending on the options available to you. Things you post in shared spaces or send to other members may be seen by their intended recipients. Please review your settings before sharing sensitive information.

## Who may help us provide the service

Authorized university staff and carefully selected outside service providers may handle information needed to host the website, deliver emails, support sign-in, protect against abuse or provide features you choose. Some providers may work outside the Philippines. We apply appropriate safeguards and may share information when required by law.

## Cookies and visitor statistics

Necessary cookies keep your account working and remember your privacy choice. Visitor statistics are optional. If you choose **Necessary only**, we do not collect optional visit records through the site's consent-based visitor feature. If you choose **Accept analytics**, those statistics may be collected while the feature is enabled. You can change your choice through **Cookie settings** without losing access to the main alumni services. Read the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy/) for details.

## Installed app and notifications

The NORSU app uses your normal account and privacy settings. Most personal information still requires an internet connection. Notifications are optional and require permission on your device. Other services involved in delivering them may receive the information needed to send the alert. You can turn notifications off in your app or device settings.

## How long information is kept

Different records may be needed for different lengths of time. Optional site-visitor records are configured for up to 30 days when regular cleanup is running. Other records may be kept longer where needed to provide services, meet university requirements, resolve disputes or follow the law. Information no longer needed is deleted or handled safely when permitted.

## Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and limit access to people who need it. No website can promise complete security. Please report any suspected misuse through the University's official channels.

## Your privacy rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may have the right to know how your information is used, see or correct records, object to certain uses, request deletion where permitted, or complain. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. See [Data Privacy Request](/legal/data-privacy-request/).

## Changes and questions

We will update this notice when needed. For questions, contact NORSU's Data Protection Officer through official channels. You may also raise concerns with the National Privacy Commission.
