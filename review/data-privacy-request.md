# Data Privacy Request

## Your privacy choices and rights

You can ask NORSU about information held about you, request access or corrections, object to certain uses, and request deletion or a copy where allowed by law. You can also report a possible privacy problem.

## How to make a request

Contact the University through the official email or contact page and use the subject **Data Privacy Request**. Please include:

1. your full name and registered email address;
2. what information or action your request concerns;
3. enough detail for us to find the relevant records; and
4. how you prefer to be contacted.

Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents by ordinary email. We may ask you to confirm your identity using a safer method before sharing or changing personal records.

## Cookies and notifications

You can change optional visitor tracking at any time using **Cookie settings**. Declining analytics does not affect normal access to the network. Your device settings control whether you receive notifications. If you would like NORSU to review visitor records, notification subscriptions or other information linked to you, include that in your request.

## What happens next

The University will review your request and reply within the period required by law. Some requests may be limited where the law requires information to be kept or where another person's rights may be affected. Where permitted, we will explain the reason for any limitation.

## If you still have concerns

Contact the NORSU Data Protection Officer through the University's official channels. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission at [privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph/).
