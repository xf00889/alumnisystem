# Community Guidelines

## Treat people with respect

Be professional and courteous with alumni, students, staff, mentors, employers, donors and visitors. Harassment, threats, hateful comments, discrimination, exploitation and repeated unwanted contact are not allowed.

## Be honest

Use your real and accurate information. Do not impersonate someone else, create misleading accounts, or falsely claim a degree, job or connection to NORSU.

## Respect other people's privacy

Do not share another person's private messages, photos, documents, payment information or contact details without permission. Do not collect alumni information in bulk or use it for unwanted advertising, surveillance or resale.

## Keep posts safe and useful

Do not share scams, spam, harmful software, unlawful content or material you have no right to use. Jobs, events, fundraisers and mentorship offers should clearly explain who is responsible. Never ask other members to disclose passwords.

## Report concerns responsibly

Use the reporting features when you see something unsafe or inappropriate. Do not make knowingly false reports. In emergencies, contact the appropriate authorities directly.

## What happens if the rules are broken

NORSU may remove content, issue warnings, restrict features or suspend accounts. Serious incidents may be referred to the appropriate university office or authorities. Contact the Office of Alumni Affairs if you need clarification or a review of a decision.
